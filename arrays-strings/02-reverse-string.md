## Problem: Reverse a String (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/

### Approach

I used two pointers, one starting from the beginning and one from the end. The characters at both positions are swapped until the pointers meet.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

I tested a normal string and a single-character string.
